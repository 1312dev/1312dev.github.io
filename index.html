1312 Wrap Design started in 2024. We work with motorsport teams, commercial vehicles and private clients.

We have our own experience in motorsport and car culture, so our designs are modern, unique and full of style.

Do you need one custom design or a long-term design partner? We are here to help. We take your project from the first idea to print-ready files.

We believe that great design should be bold, unique and full of details.

A commercial vehicle is the face of a company, so it should leave a strong impression. Of course, we also make clean and simple designs when the client needs them.

But we always ask our clients to think bigger. That’s why many of our business projects look as bright as a race car or a personal car.

Service included

01

Unique design

We talk about your ideas, colors and references to fully understand your vision. Then we write a brief and create your new, unique livery.

02

Personal presentation

Every car gets its own design presentation. If we have a 3D model of your car, we also show you a 3D visualization.

03

Ready files

When you approve the design, we prepare full-size 1:1 files for printing or cutting. This makes production fast and easy.

Team equipment

We also design merch for motorsport teams and businesses.

We prepare professional print-ready files for team tents, flags, merch, promo products and more. This helps teams and companies build a strong visual identity that people remember.
